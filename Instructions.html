PUBHORROR

Version 1.0.7 · User Guide

PubHorror is a library for the movies and shows you own, and it runs entirely on your own computer. It has a horror look, but it works for any kind of media. You can rate your films, watch whole series, play your own discs, mark jump scares, and take proper notes if you write about film.

This guide goes from installing all the way to fixing problems. The links under this box take you straight to each section.

1. Install

  1. Download PubHorror_Setup_1.0.7.exe from itch.io or GitHub.
  2. Run it. PubHorror installs just for your Windows account. It doesn't need admin rights.
  3. If your computer is missing the Edge WebView2 runtime, the installer adds it. ffmpeg, which handles video conversion, comes included.
  4. On the last screen, tick Launch PubHorror and you're in.

Want to play DVDs or Blu-rays?

You'll also need MakeMKV. It doesn't come with PubHorror, so you download it yourself from makemkv.com. If it's missing, the installer shows a checkbox that opens that page. PubHorror also offers the link the first time you try to play a disc. Install it in the default location. DVD ripping is free. Blu-ray needs a MakeMKV key once its trial runs out.

Upgrading? Run the new installer over the old one. Your library, notes and posters are kept.
Uninstalling? Your data, library and posters folders are left behind on purpose so you can't lose your library by accident. Delete them yourself if you want everything gone.

2. Add Your Movies

Click ＋ in the toolbar. You get four options:

Works with .mp4 .mkv .avi .mov .webm .m4v .m4a .wav .ac3. Your files stay where they are. PubHorror only remembers where they live.

Source types

Each title is one of these: File (a video on your computer), DVD (a disc), Series (a folder of episodes), or Wishlist (something you want but don't own yet).

3. Details, Posters & Trailers

To edit a title, click its ✏ button or right-click its poster.

  • 🎬 Auto-fill TMDB looks the movie up and fills in the title, year, director, cast, genres, description and poster. If several films match, you pick the right one. It works straight away. If it ever stops working, get your own free key at themoviedb.org and paste it into ⚙️ Settings.
  • 🏛 Look up on Wikidata adds the studio, country of origin, Wikidata ID and IMDb ID. Your citations use these.
  • Upload Poster lets you use your own poster image instead.
  • Edition records which version you watched: the cut, aspect ratio, release or restoration, region and format.
  • Content warnings: type them separated by commas, like Gore, Flashing Lights, Animal Death. A warning screen with Play Anyway and Cancel shows up before the movie plays.
  • Trailers play inside the app whenever TMDB has one listed.

4. Watching

  • Click a poster to play it. A series opens with its episode list next to the player, at the episode you were last on. If you stop partway, the 🎬 Resume button in the toolbar takes you back to that spot.
  • Watched and in-progress status updates on its own as you watch.
  • Volume is remembered between sessions.
  • Some formats, like AC3 or DTS audio and DVD video, won't play in the player as they are. PubHorror converts them for you the first time you press play. This can take a few minutes on a long movie, but it only happens once per file.

5. Series

  • Point Add Series at the show's folder. Episodes are sorted for you, and season folders named like Season 1, S01 or Series 2 are recognized.
  • Every episode has its own progress bar and watched tick.
  • When an episode ends, an Up next countdown starts. Hit ▶ Play Now to skip the wait or ✕ Cancel to stop there. There's also a Next ⏭ button in the player.
  • Added new episodes to the folder? Click 🔄 Rescan on the series' edit screen, or 🔄 Rescan in the toolbar to update every series at once.

6. Subtitles

  • PubHorror finds subtitles built into the video, and subtitle files sitting next to it with the same name (.srt, .vtt, .ass, .ssa).
  • Click CC in the player to pick a track, or load a subtitle file yourself.
  • Out of sync? The Sync buttons in the CC menu move subtitles by half a second. If the words appear too late, press −0.5s.
  • A− and A+ change the text size. Your size is remembered.

7. Playing Discs

The player can't play a disc directly, so the first time you play one, PubHorror makes a copy on your computer. After that, the disc can stay on the shelf.

  1. Make sure MakeMKV is installed.
  2. Put the disc in and choose ＋ → 💿 Add Disc…. Pick the drive. Each drive shows whether it holds a DVD, a Blu-ray, or something PubHorror can't play.
  3. Press play. PubHorror asks to rip the disc. Say yes.
  4. Wait. A rip usually takes 15 to 40 minutes, and then it converts for a little while longer. You can close the player while this runs.
  5. When it finishes, the movie plays straight from your library, without the disc.
Low on space? A ripped DVD takes several GB. Rips are saved in library\dvd_rips inside the PubHorror folder. To save them somewhere else, close PubHorror, open data\config.json in Notepad, and add a line like "dvd_rips_path": "D:/dvd_rips", just after the opening {.

8. Organizing & Personalizing

  • Search finds titles, directors and cast.
  • Filter by collection, by watch status (unwatched, watching, watched), by star rating, or by clicking the genre chips.
  • Sort A→Z, Z→A, newest, oldest, top rated, or grouped by collection.
  • Rate anything from 1 to 5 stars.
  • 📚 Collections: make your own lists (Slashers, October 2026, Watched with friends…). A movie can be in as many as you like. Choose collections for a movie on its edit screen.
  • 🎲 Random picks something for you when you can't decide.
  • 📊 Stats shows total titles, how many you've watched, your average rating and your top category.
  • The ⊞ slider resizes the posters and the A slider resizes the text.
  • Themes: Horror, Midnight, Forest, Classic, or 🎨 Custom with your own accent, highlight and background colors.

On a narrow window, some toolbar buttons move into the More ▾ menu.

9. Notes & Research Tools

Made for reviews, essays, coursework, or anyone who likes to take notes while they watch.

Timestamped notes

Type a note while watching and it's pinned to that moment. Notes on episodes stay with that episode.

Tags

Tag notes as cinematography, sound, score, editing, performance, theme, symbolism, production design, continuity or context. Or type your own.

Passage notes

Press ⏱ In where a scene starts, then Add Note where it ends. The note covers the whole stretch.

Jump scares

Hit 💀 to mark one. A skull appears on the progress bar at each one.

Search every note

🔎 Annotations searches the notes across your whole library by text, title or tag.

Citations

Turn a note into a Chicago or MLA citation, with the director, studio, year, edition and timestamp included.

Export notes

Save a film's notes as .txt, .md or .csv.

Export clips

Save a passage note as an .mp4 clip. A _source.txt file saved next to it records the film, passage, edition, tag, note and citation.

Tip: fill in the Edition fields and run the Wikidata lookup before you cite. The more details a title has, the more complete its citations are.

10. Backups

  • PubHorror saves a backup every time it starts and keeps the 10 most recent.
  • To roll back, open ⚙️ Settings, look under Recent Auto-Backups, and restore one.
  • 📤 Export Backup saves your whole library (movies, ratings, collections and settings) to one file. 📥 Import Backup loads it back in, on this computer or a new one.
Moving to a new computer: Export Backup on the old one, install PubHorror on the new one, then Import Backup. If your videos are in a different place on the new computer, edit each title and browse to its new location.

Troubleshooting

PubHorror opens to a blank white window

The WebView2 runtime is missing or broken. Download the "Evergreen Bootstrapper" from Microsoft's WebView2 page, run it, then reopen PubHorror.

A video has no sound, or won't play

Its format probably needs converting, and that happens on its own when you press play. If it keeps failing:

  • Check that ffmpeg.exe and ffprobe.exe are in the PubHorror folder. Reinstalling puts them back.
  • Check that you have free disk space. Converting makes a new copy of the file.
  • data\ffmpeg_last.log has the details.
It says "MakeMKV not found"

Install MakeMKV from makemkv.com and keep the default install location. PubHorror looks for it there. Then press play again.

The disc shows up but won't rip
  • Open MakeMKV on its own and see whether it can read the disc. If MakeMKV can't, PubHorror can't either.
  • Update MakeMKV. Old versions stop working on newer discs.
  • Blu-rays need a MakeMKV key once the trial ends.
  • Clean the disc. Scratches cause a lot of failed rips.
  • data\rip_log.txt shows what went wrong.
A rip or conversion failed because the disk is full

Free up some space, or send rips to another drive (see the tip under Playing Discs). Then press play again. If the rip itself already finished, PubHorror only redoes the conversion, so you won't wait for the disc again.

TMDB auto-fill finds nothing

Try the title without the year, or check the spelling. If it never finds anything at all, get a free key at themoviedb.org and paste it into ⚙️ Settings → TMDB API Key.

Subtitles are out of sync, or too big or small

Click CC in the player. Sync moves them by half a second, and A− and A+ change the size.

A series is missing new episodes

Click 🔄 Rescan, either on the series' edit screen or in the toolbar.

A movie says its file can't be found

The video was moved, renamed, or is on a drive that isn't plugged in. Reconnect the drive, or edit the title and browse to where the file is now.

My library is empty after a crash

Your video files are fine. Open ⚙️ Settings and restore one of the Recent Auto-Backups.

Still stuck? Ask in the Discord. It helps to paste in what's in data\ffmpeg_last.log or data\rip_log.txt.
