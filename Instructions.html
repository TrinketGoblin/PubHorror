PUBHORROR

Version 1.0.5 · The Scholar Update

PubHorror is a program built to watch home movies entirely locally! While it features a horror aesthetic, it is designed for any and all media, not just horror. Rate your films, watch entire series, play your own discs, and mark jump scares to your heart’s content. If you write about film, the new notes tools are for you.

Content warnings and Collections are out now. Check the Discord to see what’s coming next.

Step 1: Install

  • Download the installer from the latest release and run it. PubHorror installs for your user account, with no admin needed.
  • The installer sets up the Edge WebView2 runtime if you don’t have it, and includes ffmpeg.
  • To play discs you also need MakeMKV, which you download yourself from makemkv.com. The installer and the app both offer to open that page. Ripping DVDs with it is free; Blu-ray needs a MakeMKV key.

Step 2: Link Your Files

  • Open Settings (⚙️) and click Set Library Path.
  • Select the folder containing your media files (Movies, TV Shows, or Home Videos).
  • Add a free TMDB API key in settings to enable automatic metadata, posters, and trailers.

Step 3: Scan & Import

  • Click the Plus (+) icon to begin adding media.
  • Use Import Folder to automatically index your directory, or add a single movie, a series folder, or a disc.
  • Supported formats include .mp4, .mkv, .avi, .mov, and .ac3. Audio and video your player can’t handle are converted for you.
  • Series pick up their episodes automatically, track progress per episode, and offer an Up Next countdown.

Step 4: Play Your Discs

  • Click Add Disc and pick the drive. DVDs and Blu-rays are both detected.
  • The first time you play a disc title, PubHorror rips it with MakeMKV. Expect 15 to 40 minutes.
  • After that, the movie plays from your library without the disc in the drive.

Step 5: Track & Personalize

  • Mark Jump Scares and take time-stamped notes on your films.
  • Sort movies into your own Collections, and set Content Warnings that show before a movie plays.
  • Adjust the Font Size Slider for the best viewing experience.
  • Switch between Horror, Midnight, Forest, and Classic themes, or build your own colors.

For Film Writers & Students

Tagged notes

Tag notes as cinematography, sound, score, editing, theme, and more, or type your own tags. Mark a start and end to note a whole passage.

Search every note

Search all of your annotations across the whole library by text, title, or tag.

Citations

Turn a note into a Chicago or MLA citation that includes the director, studio, year, edition, and timestamp.

Edition details

Record which cut, aspect ratio, release, region, and format you watched.

Export notes

Save your notes as .txt, .md, or .csv.

Export clips

Cut a noted passage out as an .mp4. A source file with the film, passage, edition, and citation is saved next to it.

Clip export uses ffmpeg, which the installer puts next to PubHorror.

Your Library Stays Safe

  • PubHorror saves a backup every time it starts and keeps the 10 most recent.
  • Export a full backup to a file, or import one to move your library to another computer.
  • Everything runs on your machine. Nothing about your library is uploaded anywhere.
